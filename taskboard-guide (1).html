01

What the Task Board is

Every coordinator is responsible for a set of learners — the batches where they're listed as the mainCoordinator. Those learners drift in and out of states that need a phone call: a welcome call that hasn't happened yet, a CCT check-in that's due, a learner who's gone quiet on Flexi, a placement follow-up, a missed inbound call that needs calling back. Today, spotting all of that meant checking several places by hand.

The Task Board watches those signals for you. Each night it looks across every learner a coordinator owns, works out who needs a call and why, and lays it out as a single ranked queue for the next day. Coordinators work the queue; admins watch it across the whole team, step in on anything stuck, and tune how the board behaves.

Welcome call CCT follow-up 15-day / fortnightly check-in Attendance drop Placement follow-up Flexi non-engagement Live-eval gap Missed inbound callback Manual callback

Scope. The Task Board is switched on for Zenclass programs only — it doesn't appear for B2B or B2I business units.

02

The daily cycle

A task's life runs in one direction, from a signal on a learner's record to a closed call in a coordinator's history. This is the shape of it, every day.

Overnight

Signals detected

The board checks every owned learner against welcome, CCT, attendance, placement, Flexi and live-eval rules, plus Exotel's missed-call log.

03:15 IST

Board built

Tasks are created or updated per coordinator and sorted into priority lanes. Any missed run is caught up the next time the app boots.

During the day

Coordinator works it

Calls, completes, reschedules, or hands off tasks from their board — see the Coordinator walkthrough below.

On each call

Outcome logged

Connected calls close the task. No-answers count toward RNR. Everything is tied back to the exact task, not just the learner.

Ongoing

Admin oversight

Escalations, team load, and trends stay visible to admins, who can retune the rules or trigger a rebuild at any time.

COORDINATOR Owns the learners in their mainCoordinator batches

Working the board

A coordinator's day starts on the Board tab. It's a single queue, split into three lanes so the most time-sensitive calls surface first.

Priority 1

Time-critical: welcome calls close to their deadline, RNR retries, and missed-inbound callbacks owed the same day.

Priority 2–3

Due but not urgent: CCT and fortnightly check-ins, Flexi and live-eval gaps, placement follow-ups.

Over cap

Tasks that didn't fit today's calling capacity and rolled over. History is preserved, nothing is lost.

1

Scan the top cards

The board opens with counts for welcome-due, CCT push, 15-day due, RNR/missed, and Flexi at-risk, so you know where today's load is concentrated before diving in.

2

Search or filter

Find a specific learner or batch, or filter the queue down to a task type — Priority 1, RNR retries, rolled-over tasks — when you want to work in one category at a time.

3

Open a call detail

Click a learner's name to see their info, recent calls, where they stand on CCT/RNR timing, activity history, and prior comments — everything you need before you dial.

4

Make the call

Call from the row or the detail page places the call through Exotel and tags it with the task, so the outcome closes the right task automatically — no manual matching.

5

Log the outcome

Connected and resolved → the task closes itself once the outcome comes back. Talked to the learner outside Exotel (WhatsApp, personal phone)? Mark it Complete yourself. Need more time? Reschedule to a later date without losing the task's history.

6

Handle the exceptions

Learner asked to be called at a specific time — set a preferred call window or schedule a callback. Learner shouldn't be contacted — mark do not call, which moves them to Excluded. Stuck or needs admin's input — escalate, and it moves off your board into the admin queue.

The other two tabs

Board

Your working queue for today, sorted into the three lanes above.

Excluded

Learners currently suppressed from calling — suspended, on hold, marked do-not-call, past CCT sunset, or without a coordinator. If the reason is do-not-call, you can re-enable calls from here.

Escalated

Calls you've sent to admin. Read-only while admin owns them, and they don't count toward your cap or roll over while escalated.

ADMIN Oversees every coordinator's board and tunes how it behaves

Running the program

Admins don't work individual calls day to day — their view is the team, the queue health, and the rules that generate tasks in the first place.

1

Check the Dashboard

Total learners in scope, calls completed, team capacity, breaches, coordinators on leave, and trend charts over time — the health of the whole program at a glance.

2

Clear Escalations

See what's open, what got resolved today, and what got sent back. On any escalated call, you can Call it yourself, Resolve it, or Return it to the coordinator.

3

Review Coordinators

A coordinator-wise table you can search, sort, and export. Click a coordinator to drill into their board exactly as they see it — useful for spot checks or coaching.

4

Adjust Settings

Change calling caps, CCT and welcome-call windows, RNR rules, working hours, and Flexi/live-eval thresholds. Anything you change here shapes what the next board build generates.

5

Rebuild when needed

Trigger Rebuild Today's Boards after a settings change, or whenever boards need refreshing outside the nightly run. Give it a few minutes to finish before checking a coordinator's board.

Escalations don't sit idle. Every escalated task stays visible on the admin Escalations screen until it's resolved or returned — nothing disappears once a coordinator hands it off.

Who can see what

Access is permission-based, with one built-in exception: a coordinator who owns at least one mainCoordinator batch sees the Task Board nav even without the view permission explicitly assigned.

See your own board.

taskBoard.adminview

Team dashboard, escalation queue, coordinators list, and drill-down into any coordinator's board.

taskBoard.config

Settings screen and triggering a rebuild.

05

Common questions

My board looks empty — is something broken?

Probably not. Boards fill after the nightly build or an admin-triggered rebuild — if you were just added as a coordinator or a batch just started, ask an admin to rebuild.

I called a learner but the task didn't close.

The task only closes automatically when the call was placed from inside the Task Board, so the outcome carries the task ID back with it. A call placed from elsewhere in the app won't be linked — mark it Complete by hand instead.

Why can't I edit an escalated call?

Once escalated, admin owns it. It stays read-only on your board and doesn't count toward your cap until it's resolved or returned to you.

I don't see Task Board in my sidebar at all.

Check that the program is a Zenclass business unit (Task Board isn't enabled for B2B/B2I), and that you either hold the view permission or own a mainCoordinator batch.

How often does the board refresh?

Automatically every night at 03:15 IST. Admins can also force an immediate rebuild from Settings after changing a rule.
